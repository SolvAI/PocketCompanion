PocketCompanion is a private AI companion that lives entirely on your phone. Talk to her by voice, type, or show her a photo, and she answers out loud with a realistic, lip-synced avatar and big word-by-word subtitles.

Everything runs on your phone's CPU: the language model, speech recognition, the voice and the avatar. After a one-time model download (about 5 GB, Wi-Fi recommended) it works in airplane mode. No account, no subscription, no analytics, and your conversations never leave the device.

WHAT SHE CAN DO
• Voice chat: hold the button and talk. Live transcription shows what she understood while you are still speaking.
• Text chat: type a message and she answers out loud, with word-by-word subtitles.
• Photos: attach a picture and she describes and reacts to what she sees.
• Lip sync: 11 realistic digital humans whose mouths move with every word.
• 14 natural voices: American, British, Japanese-accented and Mandarin-accented.
• Make her yours: rewrite her instructions (system prompt) in Settings to change her personality.

UNDER THE HOOD
• Language model: Gemma 4 E2B (abliterated, Q4_K_M) with vision, through llama.cpp
• Speech recognition: NVIDIA Nemotron Speech Streaming 0.6B, through sherpa-onnx
• Voice: Kokoro 82M, through sherpa-onnx
• Avatar: Duix-Mobile by duix.com (Powered by Duix.com)

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 10 or newer, 64-bit ARM
• 8 GB of RAM minimum, 12 GB recommended
• About 6 GB of free storage

CONTENT NOTICE
The default model has had its refusals removed, so it can say things a filtered assistant would not. Intended for adults.
