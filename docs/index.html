Questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The first start downloads the models (about 5 GB). After that, the language model, speech recognition, voice and avatar all run on your phone, and you can use it in airplane mode.

Is it an offline alternative to Character.AI or Replika?

It covers similar ground, a companion with a personality you can talk to, but nothing is sent to a server and there is no account. It does not yet remember past conversations.

Which phones can run it?

64-bit Android 10+ phones with at least 8 GB of RAM; 12 GB is recommended. It was developed on a POCO X8 Pro. Recent mid-range and flagship phones should work; older ones will be slower.

Does it need a GPU or NPU?

No, it runs on the CPU. GPU and NPU acceleration are on the roadmap.

Is the model filtered?

The default model is an abliterated build of Gemma 4, meaning its refusals were removed, so it answers more freely than a filtered assistant. Her instructions in Settings shape her personality and what she talks about.

Can I change her personality or the model?

Her instructions are editable in Settings. Any llama.cpp-compatible model can be used by changing a few constants and rebuilding; see the README.

Is it free?

Yes. The app code is MIT licensed. The avatar engine is duix.com's SDK under the duix.com Community License.
